# Synthetic Tamweel Demonstration Policy

This document outlines the synthetic credit score risk categories, loan approval criteria, and credit limit policies for the Tamweel platform.

## 1. Credit Score Risk Categories

The Tamweel AI credit scoring engine evaluates applicants on a scale of 0 to 100. The risk categories are defined as follows:

*   **Score Range: 80 - 100**
    *   **Risk Level:** Low Risk
    *   **Approval Likelihood:** Very High
    *   **Description:** Customers with excellent financial profiles, consistent income, and minimal debt.

*   **Score Range: 60 - 79**
    *   **Risk Level:** Medium-Low Risk
    *   **Approval Likelihood:** High
    *   **Description:** Customers with good financial stability but may have minor fluctuations in income or spending.

*   **Score Range: 40 - 59**
    *   **Risk Level:** Medium-High Risk
    *   **Approval Likelihood:** Moderate (Requires manual review or conditional terms)
    *   **Description:** Customers with elevated debt ratios or inconsistent savings.

*   **Score Range: Below 40**
    *   **Risk Level:** High Risk
    *   **Approval Likelihood:** Low (Generally Rejected)
    *   **Description:** Customers demonstrating poor repayment history or unstable financial indicators.

## 2. Loan Approval Criteria

When evaluating a loan application, the following criteria are assessed to determine final approval:

*   **Credit Score:** Must meet the minimum threshold for the requested tier.
*   **Income Stability:** The applicant must show a consistent stream of income over the past 6 months.
*   **Savings Rate:** A higher percentage of income saved monthly improves approval terms.
*   **Spending Behavior:** Excessive or erratic spending patterns may trigger conditional approvals or rejections.
*   **Repayment History:** A flawless history of repaying previous obligations is required for top-tier loans.
*   **Debt Ratio:** The ratio of existing debt to monthly income must not exceed 40% for standard approvals.

## 3. Credit Limit Policies

The maximum loan amount an applicant can be approved for is strictly determined by their credit score range, assuming all other approval criteria are met.

*   **Score Range 80 - 100:**
    *   **Maximum Loan Amount:** 10,000 JOD
    *   **Notes:** Eligible for premium interest rates and expedited processing.

*   **Score Range 60 - 79:**
    *   **Maximum Loan Amount:** 5,000 JOD
    *   **Notes:** For example, someone with a score of 65 is eligible for a maximum loan amount of 5,000 JOD.

*   **Score Range 40 - 59:**
    *   **Maximum Loan Amount:** 1,500 JOD
    *   **Notes:** Requires additional collateral or a co-signer. 

*   **Score Range Below 40:**
    *   **Maximum Loan Amount:** 0 JOD (Not eligible for a loan)
    *   **Notes:** Applicant must improve financial health before reapplying.
