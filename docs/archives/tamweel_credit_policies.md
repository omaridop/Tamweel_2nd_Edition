# Chapter 1: Alternative Data Underwriting
## Section 1.1: Micro-Business Cash Flow Evaluation
For unbanked informal sector merchants, creditworthiness must be extracted via verified digital wallet incoming ledgers. A minimum of 6 consecutive months of mobile money transaction logs is required. 
* **Rule CR-10A:** Average monthly incoming velocity must exceed 300 JOD.
* **Rule CR-10B:** Month-over-month cash flow volatility must remain below 25% to qualify for baseline micro-loans.

## Section 1.2: Telecom and Utility Metadata Utility
When formal banking records are absent, utility billing consistency acts as a primary proxy for repayment discipline.
* **Rule CR-11A:** Delinquency on mobile postpaid or electricity bills exceeding 14 days within a 90-day window triggers an automatic 50-point subtraction from the base alternative credit profile score.
